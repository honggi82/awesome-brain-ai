PREPARATION OF THE PHYSICIAN.

In view of the contagiousness of gonorrhea, the phy¬ sician should prepare himself as for any capital opera¬ tion, to protect all subsequent patients and himself against infection. To this end his arms should be bared to above the elbows, and he should wear a gown or im¬ pervious apron, or preferably both. It will be well, also, to safeguard his eyes, even in emmetropie, with goodsized spectacles during the treatment of a gonorrheic.
