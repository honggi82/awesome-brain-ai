The forces at work tending to the establishment of definite relations between the intestinal trunks, nerve-cords and body-wall are much less rapid in their effects, and in small pieces may be insufficient to restore the worm to a condition of normal form and proportions.

In heteromorphosis the factors at work seem to be of the same essential nature as those playing a part in the regeneration of normal individuals. Polarity apparently depends not upon a molecular condition of the tissues, but upon simple, somewhat gross tissue relations.
