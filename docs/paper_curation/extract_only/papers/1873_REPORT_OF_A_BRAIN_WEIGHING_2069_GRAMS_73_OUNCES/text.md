First.—It should be completely buried, i. e., placed entirely subperitoneal, care being taken to catch up the muscular coat of the cecum at several places, but especially at the mesoappendix and white line points.

The average weight of the human brain, according to Quain, is 1,400 grams in the male and 1,274 grams in the female. In examining 559 male brains, Bischoff found 59 that weighed more than 1,501 grams and 18 that weighed more than 1,601 grams, and one that weighed 1,925 grams.
