At the present time I apply Harrington's solution to every part of the cut surface of the carcinomatous mass that is being removed; the growth is excised as rapidly and as carefully as possible, after the infected lymphatics and their periglandular tissue have been excised. After stoppage of hemorrhage in the wound the solution is applied to the entire field for one-half to one minute, depending on the character of the surface. In delicate flap operations the solution is very carefully applied. It is essential that all parts of the wound be touched with the solution.
