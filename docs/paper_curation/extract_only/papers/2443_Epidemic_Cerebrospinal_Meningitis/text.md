It is a curious fact that the majority of writers on this disease are ignorant ot its existence in India, the writer of the article in Allbutt's System is as ignorant

as is the present author of the book under review. Yet a reference to the volumes of the Indian Medical Gazette or the first volume of the Journal of Hygiene would have enabled him to write a more correct history of the disease rather than blindly follow Hirsch's history from his splendid but now out of date book on Geographical Pathology.

After all this is a minor blemish, for otherwise the book gives a singularly full account of the clinical history, the laboratory diagnosis, the blood-pressure and the treatment by serum, etc., of this highly fatal disease.

The book is one which should be in all

medical libraries and we can strongly recommend it.
