SUMMARY.

The second colorimetric part of the test is better omitted and the estimate of the motor power of the stomach based on the number of c.c. of residue found at the end of one-half hour.

We may answer the question whether it is better than older methods of testing gastric motility by saying that it is simpler than most; makes little demand on the appetite, and is very easy on the stomach. It is readily used in patients who are vomiting, or who are on a very simple light diet, where a big meal is uncomfortable or impossible. The sources of error are few and probably unimportant and no considerable disturbance of motility escapes detection. It is an easy method of following results of treatment of gastric stasis.
