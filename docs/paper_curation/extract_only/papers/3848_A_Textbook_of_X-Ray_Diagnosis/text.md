There is a comprehensive discussion, with appropriate illustrations, of all the pathological changes occurring in benign and malignant disease, which will be of much assistance in assessment of the various changes seen in glandular metastatic deposits. There are also chapters on isotope lymphography and intralymphatic therapy.

The first edition of Professor Illingworth's book appeared in 1960. Now, only ten years later, the fourth edition is available - this is a sure sign of the book's success and popularity. With each edition there has been a rearrangement of the material and addition of recent references, and the retention of a strong personal flavour. Of all of them, this fourth edition is perhaps the most characteristic of the author, and many will appreciate his philosophical comments which accompany the crisp text, packed full of facts, opinions and references.

The authors are to be congratulated on the excellent coverage of their subject; the book deserves a place in every radiological library for the guidance of all who undertake this branch of radiology, the indications for which are now becoming more clearly defined and, as a consequence, more widely used in the control of pelvic and genital malignant disease and the reticuloses. R A KEMP HARPER

It is a tribute to the personal influence of Arnold Gesell that a relatively brief period in his clinic inspired Professor Illingworth to make this subject the keystone of his professional life. Gesell's developmental techniques have been expanded and applied in a busy clinic situation where emphasis is upon the detection of developmental deviations in infancy and the pre-school period. Professor Illingworth describes in considerable detail his technique of developmental diagnosis andwarnsthe unwaryand inexperienced about the many pitfalls. His clinical skill is readily appreciated as he relates how he makes quick developmental examinations in a difficult busy clinic. This may be possible for a person of his experience, but the neophyte usually requires guidance and opportunities to perform more meticulous standardized test procedures in appropriately planned situations.
