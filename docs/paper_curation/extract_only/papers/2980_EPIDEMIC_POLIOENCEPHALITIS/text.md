###### Prophylaxis.

As regards the prophylaxis of malaria, I hold, as do many other medical men, that the daily dose of quinine is not a prophylaxis against malaria; recent investigations and experiments on this question by Lieut.-Colonel Anderson, I.M.S.,3 on gaol populations, showed that quinine as a prophylactic was useless and a distinct wasteof public money, for the price now of quinine is enormous.
